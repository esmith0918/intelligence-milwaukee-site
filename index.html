AI made simple, for every neighbor.

Doctor visits, jobs and benefits now start online, and more of them use AI. Intelligence Milwaukee is a new community program that will train trusted neighbors to show you how, step by step. No jargon. No judgment.

Get updatesSee the plan

Life now has a digital front door

Seeing your doctor, applying for a job and getting benefits all start online now. Many of our neighbors get stuck at that door. Most tech programs in Milwaukee are built for businesses or people chasing tech jobs. We are building one for everyday people who just need to get through.

+
HealthLog in to patient portals like MyChart, read test results, message your doctor and book visits.
✓
JobsBuild a resume, fill out online applications and practice for interviews, with AI as a helper.
$
BenefitsGet through online forms and understand confusing letters, step by step.

Our plan

This is what we are building. Classes have not started yet.

1

Train AI navigators

We plan to train and pay trusted community members to teach. People learn best from someone who has been where they are.
2

Teach real tasks

No lectures. You bring your phone and the thing you are stuck on, and we work through it together. We will also teach how to use AI safely.
3

Meet you where you are

Clinics, libraries, community centers and family settings. Small groups and one-on-one help.
4

Learn by doing

Hands-on practice and simple games make it stick, so you can do it yourself next time.
5

Push for easier systems

We will track where people get stuck, then share that with hospitals, employers and agencies so their front doors get easier for everyone.

Who it's for

Anyone in Milwaukee who feels left behind by technology, starting on the North Side.

  • Parents and caregivers
  • Older adults
  • Neighbors returning home from incarceration
  • Youth and families
